# DWD (2024) – Dunkelflaute in central Europe

**Title:** Climate change unlikely to increase Dunkelflaute events in central Europe
**Source:** Clean Energy Wire (CLEW), December 2024
**Institution:** Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) – Germany's official weather service

## Key Findings
- Average: 8.2 days/year of "High Central Europe" pattern
- 2 extreme events/year: renewable output <10% for 48+ hours
- Winter 2011/12: 23 days (anomaly)
- Grid effect: less wind in Germany = more in Scandinavia/Balkans
- European grid: reduces to 0.2 events/year

## Use in Project
Baseline for comparison – model checks if 2026 is normal or abnormal.
